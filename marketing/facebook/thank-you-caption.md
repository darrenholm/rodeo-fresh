# Facebook Post — Weekend Thank You

**Image:** none required — text-only post (a few event photos from the weekend work well if available)

> As we catch up on sleep after an incredible weekend, we have so many people to thank for making it happen. 🤠

> #HolmdaleProRodeo #WalkertonOntario #Rodeo #BruceCounty #SupportLocal

## Posting tips

- Pair with 2-3 candid photos from the weekend (crowd, sponsors' booth, volunteers, or rodeo action) if available — text-only posts get less reach than photo posts.
- Best posting windows for event pages: weekday evenings (6-9 PM) or Saturday morning, ideally within a day or two of the event while it's fresh.
- Tag sponsor pages where possible so they're notified and can reshare.
